# Narrative 

## Act I

Te (I think 12-13 is a good age) and her mom travel away from their home, away from her friends and everything she's used to. They're going a place her dad used to say was a "special place", the way the world could be.
They wind up on the outskirts of the city, among abandoned buildings and faulty infrastructure. Te is amazed to see people living here--building, planting gardens, teaching kids outside. It doesn't look like any "special place" she's ever heard of. It looks rundown and unlivable.

But there is more to it. Her mother and father are scientists. They have proposed a way of sustainable living that could be adopted anywhere, in any city, and they have the tentative consent of the government to use an abandoned portion of the city for their "experiment".
Unfortunately, Te's dad was never able to get to this point. His health failed him just before they arrived and it's now up to Te's mom to lead a few other scientists to help create this community. (As a side note that may be inferred, but not specifically said, the dad's illness may have been due to environmental factors-severe asthma or something of that nature--and a lack of funds to assist him. His death is the mom's reason to pull the trigger on an "experiment" that might have seemed precarious at first)
But there aren't just scientists here. They are interested in sustainability, but also community effort. Outside of the scientists and those who initially signed up for this commune, they have opened their doors to ANYONE who needs a place to stay or assistance.

This isn't entirely accepted by everyone. And some of the people who have decided to come to this commune are not here out of the goodness of their hearts, but out of desperation. There is tension is this newly growing community about what it will mean to SHARE resources and BUILD together to benefit EVERYONE. A concept that isn't entirely understandable to the every day capitalist.

That's why, Te's mom says, Te has to be an example. They both do.

Te...well, let's say Te isn't the most giving kid. She's not interested in sharing. Her stuff is her stuff. She doesn't know these random people and THIS PLACE IS TOO MUCH FREAKING WORK. IT SUCKS.

This first half is exploration. Te is given tasks by her mom to help the community. She may or may not adhere to them (or is that too much?)She gets to know the problems of the people around her as well as some issue that is causing a strain on creating a sustainable community--filtered water?
