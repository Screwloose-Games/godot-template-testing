# Story / Outline

Basing inspiration off of Hurricane Helen and some of the attempts to counter vulnerable communities prone to floods in Atlanta.

## Brief Backstory

Kai, Dad, and Te used to live here, but a horrible flood forced them out and they never fully recovered economically, traveling from place to place in the city. The Dad gets sick as a result of the flood and his illness lasts for years. Nevertheless, the parents decide to try and create a better future for their daughter.They fundraise enough money and create a collective of people with the same mission statement and different skills--I'm horrible at names so I'm just calling them the Collective right now. They come back to the area that was flooded and intend to repurpose the city so it's better suited for flooding (see left) with their eventual goal to be: creating artificial wetlands, smart surfaces, super trees, etc. A "sponge city" that uses vegetation and geography to SOAK up rain water and toxins.
