# Gameplay Ideation

## List of Gameplay Ideas in Priority Order

1. Day / Night Cycle (so that solar energy generation needs storage)
1. Climate system
1. Weather
1. Rain
1. Temperature
1. You can talk to people.
1. There are about 3-6 people in the community other than the main character (scope?)
1. There are dialog options.
1. Options result in different outcomes, but they do not necessarily have actual branching (Mainly to save scope).
1. Dialog sequences are typically less than or equal to 2 dialogues per character, less than or equal to 4 total when you talk to a character.
1. Dialogues include VO
1. A new character can join the community after game start.
1. You control a character that can walk around.
1. There are metrics that are tracked for the community as a whole (water, power, food)
1. You can use the mouse to do everything (Makes it mobile-ready)
1. There are buildings/objects
1. You can place some buildings / objects
1. When you build a structure, it takes some small amount of time “building” (up to 10 seconds)
1. As you develop relationship with some people, they can build instead (it just shows up the next day to save scope)
1. Some buildings/objects provide some functional benefit
1. Some can be placed.
