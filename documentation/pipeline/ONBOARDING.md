# Workflow

## Coordinate working boundaries within disciplines

Meet and align on who will do what work, generally speaking. For example, if there are a few artists, the artists will need to collaborate ahead of teh first planning meeting so it's clear who will do what work once the game jam starts.

### Why

This is important because it reduces the risk of duplicated work and missing work. It also helps others know who they need to talk to specifically. It also helps us control scope so each person doing a particular slice of work can better evaluate a more narrow slice. It also influences the design discussions because an individual who is more impacted by a decision gets more say in that decision. For example, if a design decision would require significant programming (creatinga  simulation game) then the programmer should have more say in that decision than someone who is not a programmer.
